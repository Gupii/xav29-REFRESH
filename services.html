Upgrades & Swaps

Is your desktop lagging, running out of storage, or struggling to play the latest games? Skip the stress of buying a brand-new system. Bring in your current setup, and let’s unlock its full potential with a targeted hardware upgrade.

⚙️ How It Works

  • Bring Your Own Parts: You are welcome to supply your own components and desktop.
  • Shop the Catalog: Don't have parts yet? Browse our extensive affiliate catalog at bizgram right inside this website to choose the exact hardware upgrades you want.
  • Next-Day Turnaround: Drop off your system in the evening (7:00 PM is best!), and it will be fully ready for pickup the very next day at any time you want.

💳 Transparent Service Pricing

Our labor rates scale dynamically based on the complexity of the installation, ensuring you only pay for the work required:

  • Minor Upgrades ($50 Minimum Charge): Applies to quick, straightforward swaps. Includes: Graphics Cards (GPU), Solid State Drives/Hard Drives (SSD/HDD), Processors (CPU), or Power Supplies (PSU).
  • Motherboard Replacements ($70): Applies to a standard motherboard swap requiring total component teardown and rebuild.
  • Complex / Multi-Component Swaps (Up to $150): Applies to major system overhauls, shifting to new cases, or changing multiple parts at once (e.g., Motherboard + CPU + RAM + Cooler).

💎 Added Value & Warranty

Included FREE with our installation services:

  • Drive Cloning Service: Upgrading your storage? We will clone your old drive to your new SSD for free so you don't lose your operating system, files, or settings.
  • Component Cleaning: We will give your old components a proper clean-up during the swap to improve airflow and keep your system running cool.

Peace of Mind Warranty: You are completely covered. Hardware warranties for all newly installed parts will be fully handled directly by me, or alternatively through our affiliate bizgram, depending on the source of the component.
